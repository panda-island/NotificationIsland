# NotificationIsland Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

NotificationIsland is designed to keep notification records on the user's device.

## Data handling

- Notification titles, messages, categories, timestamps, and settings are stored locally on the device.
- NotificationIsland does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or developer-controlled server.
- The app does not directly read private notifications from other apps. Content is provided only when the user configures and runs a Shortcut action.
- Premium users may export a JSON backup. The user chooses where that file is saved or shared, and the selected storage provider handles it under its own privacy terms.
- Subscription purchases and entitlement information are processed by Apple through StoreKit. NotificationIsland does not receive or store payment-card details.

## Retention and deletion

Free users keep the 10 most recent records. Premium users control their retention period and can export backups. Users can delete individual records or clear all records inside the app. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data, except for backup files the user exported elsewhere.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/panda-island/NotificationIsland/issues).
